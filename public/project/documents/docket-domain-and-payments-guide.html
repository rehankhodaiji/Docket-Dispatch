Three parts, in order. Each step says who does it, gives the link, and ends with a check that it worked.

Part C. Take payments

Right now the signup form takes no payment and saves emails only in the visitor's own browser. This part fixes both. I have read official pages for Stripe and Razorpay. For Lemon Squeezy and Paddle I only found comparison articles, so treat their India details as unconfirmed.

C1

Pick a payment option

OptionWhat I foundWatch for
Stripe IndiaOfficial page: international USD payments are for registered Indian businesses. It lists sole proprietorships, LLPs and companies, and says the account cannot be an individual. For services, an import-export code is optional, except for American Express.One Stripe page was labelled invite only. Check at sign-up that you qualify. Fee figures I saw (about 3% for international cards) came from third-party pages.
RazorpayOfficial page: international payments are an on-request feature. You activate it in your dashboard and it may need extra documents.Documents and fees were not listed. A third-party post said about 3% plus GST. Confirm with Razorpay.
Merchant of record: Lemon Squeezy, PaddleThey collect and handle sales tax for you. Comparison sites quote 5% plus 50 cents per sale.I could not confirm whether they pay out to Indian bank accounts for a solo seller. Paddle may not accept small Indian sellers. Ask them first.

My suggestion: try Stripe first. If it will not accept you, use Razorpay. If handling sales tax worries you, ask a merchant-of-record service whether they accept you. If you choose a newsletter platform with built-in payments, it usually runs on Stripe, so the same India question applies.

C2

Get your business details ready YOU

  • Business type and name (a sole proprietorship counts as a business here, a personal account does not).
  • Your identity and tax number, and a bank account in the business's name.
  • A short description of what you sell: a monthly digest of documentary funding calls.

The exact documents are shown during sign-up and I have not seen the lists. Ask an accountant whether you need to register for goods and services tax, or anything else, before you start selling. That is outside what I can advise on.
