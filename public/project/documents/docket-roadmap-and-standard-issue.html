Six-month roadmap, the funder repository, and one standard monthly mail

Covers November 2026 to April 2027. Built so one person can run it in about 9 to 10 hours a month.

1. How many listings per issue

8 to 12, aim for 10 Never fewer than 6 and never more than 12 dated calls, plus up to 3 on a watchlist and up to 5 recently closed names. The mail generator refuses to build anything outside those limits.

Why 10, and not more

These figures are my planning estimates. Replace them with your own after two issues by timing yourself.

Assumptions: 13 minutes per call (6 to find and read the funder's page, 4 to write the card, 3 to recheck before sending). Fixed work: about 1.5 h assembling and testing, 1 h sending and replying, 1.5 h keeping the funder list current, and 3 h finding readers.

What this tells you

  • The fixed work is most of your time. Going from 10 to 20 calls costs about 2 extra hours. So the issue size is not set by your hours.
  • It is set by supply and by the reader. Real dated calls are limited (see section 4), and 10 is about what can be read in five minutes, which the site promises.
  • Padding hurts more than a short issue. A reader who sees closed or vague entries stops trusting the dates. Better a clean 6 than a stretched 12.

2. The standard mail

Every issue is generated from one template and one data file, so it always looks and behaves the same. To make issue 2, copy emails/issues/issue-001.json to issue-002.json, replace the listings, and run node emails/build_issue.cjs issues/issue-002.json.

PartFixed rule
SubjectDocket · {Month Year}: {N} calls to watch
OpeningTwo sentences and a count for each section
Section orderClosing soon (deadline within 21 days of sending), Open now (open or rolling), Opening soon (within 60 days), On the calendar (dated, further away), Watchlist (no date yet, max 3)
Call capUp to 12 across the first four sections. A section with none is left out, never padded.
The cardSame six fields in the same order for every call: amount, deadline, who can apply, apply link, plus the funder, program, region and stage
DatesWeekday, date, time and time zone exactly as the funder gives them. "Not stated" when the page does not say. Never guessed.
Proof line"Page read {date}" and whether a person has confirmed it. The generator warns if the read date is more than 7 days before sending.
Closing section"Recently closed", names only, max 5
FooterCorrections line, "dates change" note, privacy, unsubscribe, postal address
