Cancellation and refunds

This page says how to cancel a Docket subscription and when we give a refund.

In short: you can cancel any time and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. We do not give refunds, except when we have billed you in error.

How to cancel

  • Use the link in your payment receipt email to manage your subscription, or email rehan.khodaiji@gmail.com and we will cancel it for you.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not take effect immediately.
  • You keep receiving every monthly issue until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that, the subscription ends and you are not charged again.

Refunds

  • We do not give refunds for a period that has already been paid for, including part of an Annual plan if you cancel midway through the year.
  • The exception is a billing error. For example, if you were charged twice for the same period, or charged after you had cancelled, email us and we will refund that charge.
  • If you think you were billed in error, include the email address you signed up with and the date of the charge.

Nothing on this page limits any rights you have under the law where you live.

Who takes the payment

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record. Your receipt comes from them. Refunds for billing errors are returned to the card or method you paid with.
