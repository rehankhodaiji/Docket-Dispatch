## Post B: "Three things funder pages leave out"
Channels: LinkedIn and Instagram carousel. Post on Mon 2 Nov.

Caption (LinkedIn):

I read ten documentary funder pages on 17 October. Three gaps showed up again and again.

1. The year. Sundance's deadlines page says "Application opens November 17" and gives no year.
2. The time zone. Sundance's 2026 Documentary Fund close appears as 16:30 MDT and as 3:30 p.m. Pacific. Screen Australia switches between AEDT and AEST. BFI gives 1pm and no zone.
3. Who can apply. IDA's Pare Lorentz fund picks films through nominations, and its page describes no way to apply directly.

Five-minute habit: write the full date with the year, convert the zone, find the "who can apply" line.

I print the date I read each page in Docket Dispatch, a monthly list of documentary funding calls: https://www.docudocket.live
