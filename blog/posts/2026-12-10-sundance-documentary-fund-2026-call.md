The Sundance Institute Documentary Fund took applications for four weeks in the spring of 2026. This post collects what that call said, so you can prepare before the next one. Sundance has not announced the next window.

## What the fund supports

The application page says the fund supports "independent, feature-length documentary films (52 minutes and longer)" and describes support for documentaries "from development through release". It offers non-recoupable funding. The page does not state grant amounts.

## The 2026 timing

The window opened at 14:00 MDT on 18 May 2026 and closed at 16:30 MDT on 15 June 2026. The page also gives the close as 3:30 p.m. Pacific time on Monday 15 June, which is the same moment. Filmmakers will be notified about their applications in early 2027.

## Who could apply

- The page calls the fund "a global granting opportunity" and sets no country restriction.
- "Prior funding or a fiscal sponsor is not required."
- The fund prioritizes budgets under $1.2 million USD, excluding distribution and impact expenses. The page presents this as a priority, and it does not describe a hard cap.
- Creative and editorial control must be held by the project's key creative team.
- The page does not state an application fee.

## Resubmissions

The page says Sundance is unable to accept resubmissions for previously reviewed projects, unless the filmmaker has a formal written invitation from staff to reapply. If a project of yours was reviewed in an earlier call, check this rule first.

## What the page does not say

- The dates of the next window.
- Grant amounts.
- An application fee.

Sundance lists applicationsupport@sundance.org and dfp@sundance.org for questions. Its guidelines and FAQ are at sundance.org/documentary-fund.

## A related program with dates

The Documentary Film Producers Track is a separate Sundance program for producers. Its application opens at 9am Pacific on Tuesday 17 November 2026 and closes at 9am Pacific on Wednesday 10 February 2027. Applicants must be lead producers who have produced at least one documentary short and are in active production or post-production. Non-US candidates must work and live in Europe or Latin America.

## Preparing while the fund is closed

1. Read the guidelines and FAQ now, while no deadline is running.
2. Add up your budget without distribution and impact costs, and compare it with the $1.2 million priority.
3. Check whether your project was reviewed in an earlier call.
4. Confirm that your key creative team holds creative and editorial control.
5. Set a reminder to check the fund's page every month. The 2026 window lasted four weeks.
