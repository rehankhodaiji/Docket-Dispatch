Most documentary funders run on a calendar. If you know when a program opened this year, you can plan for it next year. Funders do change dates, so treat last year's window as a reminder to check the funder's page, and not as a promise. Every date below comes from the funder's own page.

## Windows that have already closed in 2026

| Funder and program | 2026 window | Who can apply |
|---|---|---|
| Telefilm Canada, Theatrical Documentary Program | Opened 8:00 AM EDT on 19 March, closed 11:59 PM EDT on 2 April | Canadian production companies with a head office in Canada |
| Berkeley Film Foundation, Documentary Grant | Closed 11:59pm PST on 13 April, with no extensions | Filmmakers who live or work in Berkeley, Emeryville, Albany, El Cerrito, Richmond, Alameda or Oakland |
| Sundance Institute, Documentary Fund | Opened 14:00 MDT on 18 May, closed 16:30 MDT on 15 June | Independent feature-length documentaries of 52 minutes or longer, with no country limit stated |
| BFI Doc Society, Development Fund Round 1 | 1pm on 24 June to 1pm on 5 August | UK filmmakers |

The Sundance Documentary Fund window was open for four weeks. Applicants will be notified in early 2027, and the next window has not been announced.

## Programs with no fixed calendar

- **Channel 4.** Its 4Producers briefs page says the commissioning teams accept pitches on a rolling, ongoing basis. Its Documentaries and Factual Entertainment brief lists no deadline.
- **ITVS Open Call.** The page says it is not currently accepting applications and gives no dates.
- **IDA Pare Lorentz Documentary Fund.** IDA's funding page says it is expected to reopen in late 2026 with a new theme. Films are chosen through nominations.

## How to use a calendar like this

- Add last year's window to your calendar about six weeks early, as a reminder to look at the funder's page.
- Check each funder's page once a month. Announcements often come close to the opening date.
- Keep a short list of funders whose next window is not announced, and review it when you plan your year.
- Copy the time zone with the date. A close at 3:30pm Pacific is a different hour in London or Sydney.
